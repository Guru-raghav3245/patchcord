Privacy

The app

Patchcord talks to your device over USB and nothing else. It makes no network connections, has no accounts, and sends no analytics or crash reports. It needs no Full Disk Access. Files you copy go straight between your Mac and your device.

This website

The site sets no cookies, has no analytics or advertising, and loads nothing from other companies: even the fonts are hosted here. It is hosted on Vercel, which, like any web host, keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, time and page requested) for a limited period to run and protect the service. I don’t access or use them to identify visitors.

Your data

I hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing to export or delete. If you email me, I use your address only to reply.
